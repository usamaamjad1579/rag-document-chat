# Northwind Robotics Employee Handbook

> Sample document for testing. Northwind Robotics is a fictional company.

## Working hours

Our core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Pakistan Standard Time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you can plan your own schedule, as long as you work 40 hours per week. Meetings should be booked inside core hours whenever possible.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after completing their probation period. Each team keeps one shared office day, chosen by the team lead, so that planning and design reviews happen in person. Remote work from outside Pakistan for more than two weeks needs written approval from HR, because it affects tax and insurance.

The company pays a monthly internet allowance of PKR 6,000 to every employee who works remotely at least one day per week.

## Probation

New employees have a probation period of three months. During probation the notice period is one week for either side. After probation it becomes one month. Your manager holds a review meeting in the last two weeks of probation.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Leave is earned monthly, so a new employee earns 1.67 days for every full month worked. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March.

Leave requests of three days or more should be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal.

## Sick leave

Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than two consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Interns

Interns work 30 hours per week and receive a monthly stipend instead of a salary. Interns get 1 day of paid leave per month of internship. Interns may work remotely one day per week from their first week. Each intern is assigned a mentor from their team, and meets the mentor at least once a week.

## Learning budget

Every full-time employee has a yearly learning budget of PKR 120,000. It can be spent on courses, certifications, books and conference tickets related to your role. Claims above PKR 40,000 need approval from your manager before purchase. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a second monitor. Laptops are replaced every three years. If equipment is lost or damaged, report it to IT within 24 hours. Personal devices may only be used for work email and chat, not for source code.

## Code of conduct

We expect respectful communication in every channel. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated. Concerns can be raised with your manager, with HR, or anonymously through the ethics form on the intranet. Reports are reviewed within five working days.
