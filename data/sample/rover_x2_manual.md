# Rover X2 User Manual

> Sample document for testing. The Rover X2 is a fictional product.

## Overview

The Rover X2 is a small warehouse robot that moves shelves of up to 120 kg between storage zones. It navigates with a 2D lidar sensor and floor markers, and it stops automatically when a person comes within 1.5 metres.

## Battery and charging

The Rover X2 uses a 48 V lithium iron phosphate battery. A full charge takes 2 hours and 30 minutes on the standard dock. A full battery lasts about 9 hours of normal operation. When the charge drops below 15 percent, the robot finishes its current task and returns to the nearest dock on its own.

Do not charge the battery below 0 °C. Charging in the cold permanently reduces battery capacity.

## Status lights

- Solid green: ready for a task.
- Blinking green: moving a shelf.
- Solid blue: charging.
- Blinking yellow: waiting because the path is blocked.
- Solid red: emergency stop is active.
- Blinking red: hardware fault. Contact support with the error code shown in the app.

## Emergency stop

Press the red button on top of the robot to stop it immediately. To resume, twist the button clockwise until it pops up, then confirm the restart in the fleet app. The robot will not resume on its own after an emergency stop.

## Maintenance

Clean the lidar window every week with a dry microfibre cloth. Check the wheels for dust and debris every month. Replace the drive wheels every 2,000 operating hours. The fleet app shows the operating hours under Settings, then Diagnostics.

## Error codes

- E101: lidar window is dirty or blocked. Clean the window and restart.
- E204: floor marker not found. Check that the marker is not damaged or covered.
- E310: motor overheated. Let the robot cool for 20 minutes before restarting.
- E402: battery temperature is too high. Move the robot to a cooler area.

## Warranty

The Rover X2 comes with a two-year warranty covering manufacturing defects. The battery is covered for three years or 3,000 charge cycles, whichever comes first. The warranty does not cover damage from loads above 120 kg or from opening the sealed battery case.
